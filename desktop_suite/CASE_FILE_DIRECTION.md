# Case File product direction and evidence boundary
Owner direction: a unified authorized identity-research and evidence workspace, not a file-hash-only utility. Preserve existing AtlasVerify contracts, privacy and suppression controls, original assets and registered providers. References supplied by owner: BeenVerified, Whitepages, Spokeo (spelling normalized), TruthFinder, Intelius, Social Catfish, TruePeopleSearch.

Target experience: a focused multi-mode workspace search, candidate review with source dates and explainable match signals (not probabilities), tabbed case records, source-linked timeline, relationship review, authorized imports, keyboard navigation, export and clear-session controls. Design around self-research, consent, legitimate authorized cases and fraud verification; do not implement unrestricted private-person tracking, sensitive-data harvesting, bulk contact exposure, automatic identity merges or biometric identity recognition. Opt-out and correction must apply to query paths, reports and retained data according to verified policy. No regulated eligibility screening. Provider licensing, privacy requirements and coverage must be independently qualified before live integration; no promises based on adapter registration.

Current source: app/api/search/route.ts searches authenticated saved subjects, excludes suppressed records, and explicitly disables external search. providers/index.ts registers external and mock adapters; registration is not live verification. lib/subject-duplicate-review.ts returns identityConfirmed:false and mergeRecommended:false. The desktop edition still only provides local cases and evidence.

This draft fixes explicit destination selection, stable unique new IDs, atomic writes, corruption-safe reads and text-safe rendering. Local evidence acquisition computes SHA-256 of an explicitly selected regular file. It does not establish identity, trusted time, digital signature validity or tamper-evident custody; the old platform-specific signature inspection is deliberately not returned by this endpoint until independently qualified. Existing historical evidence is not reassigned or deleted; duplicate case identifiers require manual review. Host/origin guards are not authentication against other local software. Native UI and full external engine integration remain unverified. Do not publish or merge.
