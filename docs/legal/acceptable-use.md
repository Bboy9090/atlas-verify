# AtlasVerify Acceptable Use Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026

AtlasVerify is a consumer/public-record research and evidence-management platform for lawful use of public, licensed, and user-supplied information.

## Permitted uses

- Finding and organizing publicly available or properly licensed records.
- Reverse phone, email, address, username, business, property, and public-web research where lawful.
- Identity verification and fraud prevention that is not used to make regulated eligibility decisions.
- Personal due diligence, reconnecting with known contacts, family-history research, and lawful case research.
- Legal or compliance research performed by authorized professionals under applicable law.
- Saving sources, confidence, freshness, conflicts, and audit evidence for later review.

## Prohibited uses

- Stalking, harassment, intimidation, doxxing, or threats.
- Unauthorized surveillance or monitoring.
- Using AtlasVerify to locate a person for violent, coercive, or abusive purposes.
- Obtaining or exposing highly sensitive credentials, passwords, authentication secrets, or protected account access.
- Discrimination or targeting based on protected characteristics.
- Reselling or redistributing AtlasVerify data except where expressly permitted by the source license and law.
- Any unlawful access, impersonation, fraud, or violation of a provider's terms.

## Consumer-reporting / FCRA boundary

AtlasVerify is **not a consumer reporting agency** and its standard consumer product is **not designed or authorized for decisions covered by the Fair Credit Reporting Act or similar eligibility laws**.

Do not use AtlasVerify standard reports to determine eligibility for:

- employment;
- housing or tenancy;
- credit or lending;
- insurance underwriting or eligibility;
- education admissions;
- government benefits; or
- any other purpose that legally requires a regulated consumer report.

A separate product and compliance program would be required before AtlasVerify could support those uses.

## Accuracy and source transparency

AtlasVerify may aggregate information from multiple sources. Public and licensed sources can be incomplete, stale, conflicting, or incorrect. Users must review source attribution, retrieval dates, confidence indicators, and conflicts before relying on a result.

AtlasVerify must not describe an uncorroborated search result as verified fact.

## Privacy and suppression

People may request suppression of data from AtlasVerify-controlled search results and indexes where applicable. Requests should collect only the minimum information necessary to identify the relevant record and verify the request.

AtlasVerify should not require a government-issued identity document by default. If additional verification is necessary, use the least intrusive method reasonably available and delete verification material after the request is completed unless retention is legally required.

## Enforcement

Accounts may be suspended or terminated for misuse. AtlasVerify may preserve and disclose records when required by valid legal process or when necessary to protect users, the service, or the public.
